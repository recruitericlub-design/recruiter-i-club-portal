# 🎨 Web Design System & Anti-AI Standards (2026 Edition)

## 1. The "Anti-AI Slop" Manifesto
Websites generated blindly by AI or mediocre templates share unmistakable hallmarks that destroy B2B trust:
- **Generic Purple/Indigo haze**: Indiscriminate radial gradients (`blur-3xl bg-purple-500/20`) without purpose.
- **Sterile Card Clones**: 3-4 identical cards in a row with a centered Lucide icon and 2 lines of generic marketing buzzwords.
- **Stock 3D Blobs / Plastic Humans**: Midjourney-style glossy abstract balls or plastic-skinned 3D models with creepy smiles.
- **Zero Information Density**: Massive empty hero sections that say "Empowering Your Future with Scalable Solutions" without any pricing, specs, or actual facts.

### The 2026 High-Craft Standard:
1. **Neo-Editorial Precision**: The site feels like a premium Swiss engineering magazine or an aerospace telemetry terminal (Linear, Vercel, Teenage Engineering, Apple Pro).
2. **Tactile Hyper-Physicality**: Sub-pixel borders (`border-white/[0.08]`), noise grain overlays, matte frosted glass (`backdrop-blur-md bg-slate-900/60`), and mechanical toggle switches.
3. **Information Asymmetry**: Rhythmic Bento Grids where one hero card is 2x2, flanked by a telemetry ticker and a 1-column interactive audit terminal.
4. **Authentic Data Artifacts**: Real numbers (UAH/EUR, days, bar pressure, ISO standard numbers, shift output norms), real blueprint dimensions, and verified telemetry.

## 2. Graphic Asset Creation Guidelines (How to Make Real Graphics in 2026)

### A. Technical Schematics & Blueprints (Vector SVG)
- **Aesthetic**: CAD drawings, laser cutter paths, wireframe orthographic projections, X-ray radiography inspection lines.
- **Palette**: Monochromatic slate (`#0f172a`), blueprint cyan (`#06b6d4`), amber measurement lines (`#f59e0b`), surgical emerald status badges (`#10b981`).
- **Details**: Dimension callouts (e.g. `← 250mm ±0.02 →`), crosshairs (`+`), grid coordinates (`[LAT 28.6139 | LON 77.2090]`), and inspection stamps.

### B. High-Fidelity Industrial Photography (Cinematic Realism)
- **Style**: 35mm film or medium-format industrial documentary (Kodak Vision3 / Arri look).
- **Lighting**: High-contrast, practical tungsten lighting mixed with cool industrial neon or welding torch flares (deep shadows, natural dust particles, real metal textures).
- **Subject**: Real workers with protective gear (ESAB masks, Wurth workwear), actual welding beads, CNC lathe chips, hydraulic fluid pressure gauges.
- **Never**: Plastic AI skin, floating purple sparkles, generic stock business handshakes in glass offices.

### C. Tactile Badges & Hardware Labels
- Render UI components like physical aluminum nameplates (anodized finish, chamfered edges, laser-etched monospace font).
- Status indicators styled as LED diodes with subtle falloff glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(16,185,129,0.5)`).

---

## 3. Typography & Micro-Hierarchy Rules
- **Headline Font**: Humanist or geometric grotesque with tight tracking (`font-extrabold tracking-tight`).
- **Telemetry / Metadata**: Monospace font (`font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-widest`) for dates, timestamps, technical specs, and serial numbers.
- **Editorial Body**: High-legibility sans (`text-slate-300 leading-relaxed`) with strong typographic hierarchy.

---

## 4. UI/UX Interaction Standards (2026)
- **Zero-Friction Discovery**: Dynamic Slide-Over Drawers for deep content rather than forcing full page navigation away from the conversion funnel.
- **Interactive Calculators**: Real-time reactive sliders that calculate ROI, legal fines, and workforce needs with instant numeric feedback.
- **Haptic / Audio Micro-Feedback**: Optional subtle mechanical clicks for toggles and telemetry scan buttons.
